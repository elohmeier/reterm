Firmware flasher

1 · Pick your frame

Before you flash

Flashing replaces the firmware on the device, including Seeed's factory firmware. If you may ever want to go back, make a full flash backup first, e.g. esptool --chip esp32s3 read-flash 0 ALL backup.bin. Seeed publishes its stock TRMNL firmware and web flasher on its Firmware Hub.

2 · Connect and install

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop — iOS and most mobile browsers cannot flash.
  • Turn the rear power switch on and connect the frame over USB-C.
  • The frame shows up as a serial port (CH341 USB bridge — on Linux typically ttyUSB0).
This browser cannot flash over USB — use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Serial access was blocked. Reload and allow the serial-port permission.

3 · First start

After flashing, press the green button (E1001) or tap a touch control (E1004): the frame shows a Wi-Fi setup QR on first use, then a one-time QR that opens the Photo Magic editor for wireless uploads.
